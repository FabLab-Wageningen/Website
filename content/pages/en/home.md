## Become a member

To work on your own, you take an introduction course of two evenings, after which you are a member for a year.

- First year (including the course): €100, under 18 and students €50
- Following years: €50 per year, under 18 and students €35

As a member you can work in the FabLab on your own every weekday between 08:00 and 18:00. In the evenings and at weekends you need a key card for the building.

Email <info@fablabwag.nl> for upcoming course dates.

---

## Volunteers wanted

FabLab Wageningen is run by volunteers, young and old, with a creative, technical or teaching background. We teach courses and workshops, maintain the machines, help our members and look after the website. No experience needed.

Drop by during the open drop-in or email <info@fablabwag.nl>.
