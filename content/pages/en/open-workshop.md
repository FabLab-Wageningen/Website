### Drop-by

Every Wednesday from 14:00 to 18:00, during the StartHub networking events, we’re there for questions and advice.

Every other Saturday afternoon, from 14:00 to 17:00, we have Open Workshop. Everybody is welcome to have a look, ask questions, discuss plans and, potentially, have a job done by FabLab members. Have a plan or a drawing? Bring it along and see what can be done! Our coffee is free.

On Saturdays, please use our side entrance, not the main entrance of Plus Ultra II. Upcoming dates are listed below.
