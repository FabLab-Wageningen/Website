FabLab Wageningen has a vinyl cutter which can cut various materials up to a width of 60 cm. The cutter has a very small, sharp and nimble knife that can be used to cut extremely precise shapes in foils of varying thickness. The machine can be used to make adhesive lettering for texts or even designs to decorate clothing. We have special foil that can be applied with our heat press to decorate clothes like t-shirts. Lettering or graphical designs to apply on a car or a billboard, or even a small print for you own coffee cup are equally possible. The cutting foil is cut from a roll, so it is possible to cut very long objects or forms.
